THE ADVENTURER'S CHRONICLE  v1.4.0 (BETA)  by Always
For Ragnarok Offline 1.4.8 or newer. Pre-renewal.

A story-driven main quest that walks you across Rune-Midgard at the right
pace for your level. For new players, and for anyone coming back after
years and wondering where to go next.


==========================================================================
 FEATURES
==========================================================================

  8 chapters, 40 quests
    Prontera, Payon, Morroc, Geffen, Al De Baran and Glast Heim, Juno,
    Rachel and Hugel, from level 1 to Odin's Temple past 99. The story is
    Rune-Midgard's own lore: the weakening seals, Geffenia, the fall of
    Glast Heim, Juperos, The Sign.

  Hunt, gather, fight
    Each chapter has a hunt, items to bring back, and a named boss waiting
    in a dungeon of that region. Party and companion kills count. Bosses
    stand out: half again as big as their kin, with a red mark that
    follows them on your minimap.

  Quest EXP that keeps you on pace
    Each finished quest gives the EXP to reach the next quest's level, so
    you're always ready for what comes next. Your companions get the same
    quest EXP, so they keep up with you.

  Chapter rewards
    Zeny, supplies, and a costume headgear of your choice (3 per chapter).

  Journal (Alt+B)
    Every chapter you've finished, retold, and what to do now, with a
    Route link that opens Navigation with the way to the next NPC, hunting
    ground or boss. Also opens from the Journal button under the minimap,
    the Adventurer's Chronicle book in your bag, or @chronicle.

  Quest window
    Each quest is in Alt+U too, with live kill counts and a Route link.

  Side Quests (optional)
    Chapter NPCs mention famous places on the way, and each becomes an
    optional Side Quest: the sea caves of Byalan Island, the Sunken Ship,
    the Sphinx, the Clock Tower's sealed floors, and RO's own quests in
    Ayothaya, the Orc's Memory, Amatsu, Kunlun and Turtle Island, guided
    step by step: every right answer named, every dead end warned about,
    every item counted.
    They never block the next chapter. Each teaches a new way to travel
    (your first boat, the port of Alberta, a ship overseas) and gives a
    choice of two costumes tied to its place, a small reward and a Passport
    stamp. Changed your mind? The giver trades your costume for the other
    one, once.
    3 stamps earn the first Passport rank, 6 the second: costumes with a
    bonus.

  MVP Ledger
    A Journal tab that fills in as people on your road mention the MVPs
    near them (the Golden Thief Bug, Moonlight Flower, Eddga, Drake...),
    with their level and where they sleep. It ticks itself when you or
    your party defeat one. Nothing on it is required.

  Veterans welcome
    A character already past Chapter 1's levels can have the story told in
    brief and start at the chapter for their level.

  Travel
    Waykeepers (setting "Waykeepers", on by default) stand in front of the
    Prontera fountain and in every town. The first time you set foot on a
    town, field or dungeon it is remembered, and any Waykeeper can send you
    back there.


==========================================================================
 NOTES
==========================================================================

  - BETA. Pre-renewal only.
  - Tested: Chapters 1 to 3. Chapters 4 to 8, the costume rewards and the
    Journal's Route link are still being tested. If something breaks,
    type @chronicle and send the chapter and step it shows, with what
    happened.
  - Best on a new character, but any character can start it: the Chapter
    1 knight offers higher-level characters a skip ahead.
  - Chapter 8 goes past level 99 with a level cap mod, and still works at
    99 without one.
  - The Chapter 4 orc hunt (gef_fild14) has a Route link from version
    1.1.2, with the app's 1.5.5 client data. Older clients' Navigation
    doesn't know that map: walk west from the orc village (gef_fild10).
  - Some places can't be walked to, so Navigation can't route there. The
    Chronicle walks you through them instead: the Culvert (volunteer with
    the Recruiter), Morroc (closed by the Continental Guard: the Kafra
    teleports you in and out), Juperos Core (the elevator puzzle) and
    Odin's Temple (the Boatman in Hugel). See "How to get there" in the
    Journal and the quest window.
  - Uses item IDs 50100-50144 and quest IDs 77001-77040 and 77111-77199.
  - The Ayothaya Side Quest is RO's own quest. Nafisa's courier hands you a pouch
    with most of what its shaman asks for (some of it is very rare at
    that level); you still gather 2 Fabric and 2 Nine Tails yourself.
  - The Orc's Memory is an instance: it needs a party of 2 or more, all
    online and Lv 30-80 (companions count).
  - Kunlun's Broken Sword is RO's longest quest, with a few answers that
    end it for good. The Journal names the right ones, and warns you if an
    earlier wrong answer already closed it.
  - Once the Chronicle is complete, it points you to Rune-Midgard's own
    legends: The Sign, Thanatos Tower, the Abyss Lakes, the Endless Tower.


==========================================================================
 INSTALL
==========================================================================

  1. Settings > Mods > Add mod from folder..., and pick this zip.
  2. Press Apply, then reopen the game.
  3. Log in: the Chronicle book is in your bag and the first quest begins.
     Talk to Sir Leonhart by the Prontera fountain.

  Settings: Settings > Mods > adventurers-chronicle
  - Quest EXP (%): 100 = exactly to the next quest's level. Lower it if
    you like to hunt the rest yourself.
  - Companions share quest EXP (on): every companion in your party gets
    the same reward, brought toward the same level as you.
  - Waykeepers (on): the travel NPCs in every town. Switch off if you
    use another warper.
  - Show the Journal button.


==========================================================================
 UNINSTALL
==========================================================================

  1. Settings > Mods: remove adventurers-chronicle (or switch it off).
  2. Press Apply, then reopen the game.

  The quests and Journal go with the mod. The Chronicle book and the costumes stop working,
  since their items no longer exist on the server.
  Your story progress is remembered: install the mod again and you carry
  on where you left off.
